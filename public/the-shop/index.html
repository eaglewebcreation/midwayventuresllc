What’s included

  • Heated

    An overhead natural gas forced-air furnace keeps the shop warm through a Wyoming winter. Paint, glue and gaskets get the warm room they need.

  • 16-foot ceilings

    Room overhead for lifting, tall racks, or a vehicle with something mounted on top.

  • 36 × 45 floor

    About 1,570 square feet inside the walls to lay out the way your work needs it.

  • Private bathroom

    A 5 × 5 ft restroom inside the building, so a long day in the shop doesn’t mean leaving it.

  • Private office

    Its own room with a door, apart from the shop floor. Size: 10 × 10 ft.

  • Big overhead door

    A clear opening about 15 ft wide and 14 ft 10 in tall, on a WiFi opener you run from your phone.

  • Water, sewer and trash included

    Plus a water spigot and a floor drain, so washing down parts and equipment is easy.

  • 220V outlet

    Plug in the welder, the compressor or other big tools, with standard 110V outlets for everything else.

Questions about the shop

How big is the shop?

36 by 45 feet on the building plans. Inside the walls it measures 35 ft 7 in by 44 ft 1½ in, about 1,570 square feet, with 16-foot ceilings.

Does the shop have a bathroom and an office?

Yes, both are private and only for the renter of the shop. Office size: 10 × 10 ft

How much is the rent?

$1,250 a month in base rent as of November 2026. The rental page adds a payment fee: about 1.75% by ACH bank draft or about 4% by card.

How big are the doors?

An overhead main door with a clear opening about 15 ft wide and 14 ft 10 in tall, on a WiFi app-controlled opener, plus a 3 ft walk-in door.

What power does the shop have?

The shop has one 220V outlet for a welder, compressor or other big tools, and standard 110V outlets for the rest.

What utilities are included?

Water, sewer and trash are included. The shop also has a water spigot and a floor drain.
